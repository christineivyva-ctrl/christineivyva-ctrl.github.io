About Christine

The Operations Partner Behind Scalable Ecommerce Growth

I'm an Ecommerce Growth & Marketplace Operations Specialist with 6 years of experience supporting Amazon Seller Central, Shopify, Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop.
