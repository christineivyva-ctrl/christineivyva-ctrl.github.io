Shopify Store Operations & Catalog Management

As part of daily ecommerce operations, I managed the administrative side of Shopify stores to ensure products remained organized, inventory stayed accurate, and customers experienced a seamless purchasing process. My work focused on maintaining operational consistency while supporting overall business growth through efficient catalog management and store administration.

Project Overview

This mock case study demonstrates the type of operational support I provide for Shopify businesses. Responsibilities included maintaining product catalogs, coordinating inventory updates, organizing collections, monitoring order workflows, and ensuring storefront information remained accurate and up to date.

Responsibilities

  • Uploaded and maintained Shopify product listings.
  • Organized collections and product categories.
  • Managed pricing updates and promotional changes.
  • Maintained SKU consistency across products.
  • Monitored inventory quantities and stock availability.
  • Supported order processing and fulfillment coordination.
  • Maintained product descriptions and ecommerce SEO fields.
  • Performed quality assurance before product launches.
  • Documented operational procedures for recurring tasks.
  • Worked alongside marketing and operations teams to ensure store accuracy.

Tools Used

  • Shopify Admin
  • Google Sheets
  • Google Drive
  • Canva
  • Slack
  • ClickUp
  • Notion

Operational Improvements

  • Improved product organization across multiple collections.
  • Reduced listing inconsistencies through standardized workflows.
  • Maintained accurate inventory synchronization.
  • Created repeatable operating procedures for product management.
  • Supported efficient fulfillment coordination.
  • Improved overall store maintenance processes.

Business Impact

Although this portfolio example contains representative business data, the operational approach reflects real ecommerce workflows. Organized catalogs, consistent inventory management, and documented systems contribute to smoother daily operations, fewer listing errors, and a better customer shopping experience.
