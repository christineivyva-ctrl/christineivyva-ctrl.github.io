Amazon Seller Central · Private Label · Marketplace Operations · Product Research · Amazon PPC

Overview

Managed end-to-end operations for Amazon private label brands by overseeing Seller Central, product research, supplier sourcing, listing creation, marketplace optimization, PPC campaign management, inventory monitoring, catalog health, and executive reporting. Supported both established products and new product launches by conducting market research, sourcing manufacturers through Alibaba, optimizing listings for Amazon SEO, and building operational systems that improved efficiency, marketplace visibility, and business decision-making.

End-to-End Ownership

This project demonstrates ownership across the complete ecommerce lifecycle—from identifying profitable product opportunities and sourcing suppliers to launching products, managing marketplace performance, optimizing advertising, monitoring operational KPIs, and reporting directly to leadership. Rather than supporting isolated tasks, the focus was building reliable operational systems that enabled the business to scale efficiently while maintaining marketplace health and operational excellence.
