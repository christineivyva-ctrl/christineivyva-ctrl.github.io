Executive Operations Dashboard & KPI Reporting

Business leaders need visibility into daily operations without spending hours gathering information. This dashboard was designed to consolidate operational metrics into a single reporting system that supports faster decision-making, improved accountability, and more efficient business operations.

Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates how executive reporting can simplify business operations by organizing critical metrics into one centralized dashboard. Instead of manually reviewing multiple spreadsheets and systems, leadership receives clear, actionable insights through structured reporting and operational documentation.

Dashboard Components

  • Executive KPI Summary
  • Sales Performance Tracking
  • Amazon Marketplace Performance
  • Inventory Health Monitoring
  • Order Fulfillment Status
  • Customer Support Metrics
  • Advertising Performance Overview
  • Weekly Operations Scorecard
  • Task & Project Status Tracker
  • Executive Notes & Action Items

Tools Used

  • Google Sheets
  • Google Workspace
  • Looker Studio
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Notion
  • ClickUp
  • Slack
  • Amazon Seller Central Reports
  • Shopify Analytics

Operational Improvements

  • Centralized multiple operational reports into one executive dashboard.
  • Reduced manual reporting through standardized KPI tracking.
  • Created repeatable reporting templates for weekly business reviews.
  • Improved visibility across inventory, sales, and operations.
  • Documented reporting workflows for long-term operational consistency.
  • Supported leadership with organized, data-driven decision making.

Key Performance Indicators Monitored

  • Total Revenue
  • Orders Processed
  • Inventory Levels
  • Buy Box Status
  • Account Health
  • ACoS
  • CTR
  • CPC
  • ROAS
  • Conversion Rate
  • Customer Response Time
  • Task Completion Rate

Business Impact

While the figures presented in this portfolio example are representative, the reporting framework reflects real-world operational practices. Executive dashboards reduce reporting time, improve operational transparency, identify issues earlier, and provide business leaders with a clearer understanding of marketplace performance and organizational health.
